Nibbling lives in your menu bar. These are its actual switches. Try them — each one changes its example.

Keep typing and nothing happens. A suggestion never goes into your text until you take it.

Read the screen

Uses the message you’re answering, so your reply can pick up the names, times and places in it. It’s read on your Mac and kept in memory; images are never saved.

Dinner at Nonna’s, 7:30?See you at Nonna’s at 7:30.

Learn my words

Picks up words you type often — project names, people’s names — and finishes them your way. It keeps the words and how often you use them, never your sentences.

Pushing the Halcyon migration tonight.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing you write, say or copy. Here is everything it does fetch or send, so you don’t have to take that on trust.

What you type, say or copy
Stays on your Mac. The AI that writes the suggestions runs on it.
What’s on your screen

The AI model
Downloaded once during setup: 2.39 GB, or 1.09 GB for the lighter one.
Updates
Nibbling checks for new versions and installs them when you step away. You can turn that off.
Usage counts
Numbers only, like how many suggestions you took, through Google Analytics for Firebase. Never what you write. Turn it off in Settings.

Install it with
one command

Apple hasn’t notarised Nibbling yet, so macOS blocks a normal download. This command installs it without that.

  1. 1Copy the command.
  2. 2Open Terminal: press ⌘ space, type Terminal, press Return.
  3. 3Paste with ⌘ V and press Return. Nibbling opens when it’s in.
Terminal 
~ % curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dbhardwaj99/nibbling/main/install.sh | bash
Read the script first

What it does: downloads Nibbling from its public releases page, checks it hasn’t changed on the way, puts it in Applications and opens it. It never asks for your password.

If macOS says “Possible malware, Paste blocked”: that’s about where the text was copied from, not what it does. Choose Paste Anyway.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. To update later, run the same command again.

Questions

Which apps does it work in?

Most places you type on your Mac: Mail, Messages, Slack, Notes and text boxes on web pages. It stays out of password fields, Terminal, Passwords and a browser’s own address bar, and you can leave out any app with one switch.

Does it work in my language?

It writes in whatever language you’re writing in, and mixes like Hinglish too. There’s nothing to set.

What does my Mac need?

Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later; dictation needs macOS 26. The model takes 2.39 GB of space, or 1.09 GB for the lighter one, which setup recommends on Macs with 8 GB of memory.

How do I remove it?

Quit Nibbling and move it to the Trash. To remove the model and learned words too, move ~/Library/Application Support/Autocompleter to the Trash.
